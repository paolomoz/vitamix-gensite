TROUBLESHOOTING GUIDE

Quick Cleaning Routine for Stubborn Residue

1

Self‑Clean

Fill the container halfway with warm water, add one drop of dish soap, secure the lid, start the machine, increase to highest speed, and blend for 60 seconds.

Always unplug before cleaning or handling the base
2

Rinse & Dry

Pour out the solution, rinse the container thoroughly with warm water, and let it air‑dry upside down.

3

Deep Clean

Pour 1 cup white vinegar into the container, fill halfway with warm water, soak for several hours or overnight, then scrub with a soft pot scrubber. Run the self‑clean cycle again.

Handle blades carefully; use a long‑handle brush and avoid touching the blade assembly
4

Final Check

Inspect the blade area for any remaining debris; if residue persists, repeat the deep‑clean soak.
