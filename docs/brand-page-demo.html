Which Vitamix is best for smoothies?
The A3500 is the strongest choice for daily smoothie makers. Its dedicated Smoothie program runs a timed, multi-speed cycle optimized for whole fruits, leafy greens, and ice. Self-Detect technology adjusts the program based on which container you use. If you primarily make smoothies and want the most effortless experience, the A3500 is the right machine. The V1200 and E310 both blend smoothies well but require manual speed management.
Can I blend frozen fruit without adding liquid?
You can, but a small amount of liquid helps the vortex form correctly and extends the life of the motor. Even two to four ounces of water, juice, or plant milk is enough. Without any liquid, the blades can cavitate — spinning without contact — which leaves chunks and puts unnecessary strain on the drive system. A splash goes a long way.
How do I clean the Vitamix after a smoothie?
The self-clean method takes under a minute. Add warm water to the halfway mark, a drop of dish soap, secure the lid, and run on high for 30 to 60 seconds. Pour out, rinse once, and air dry. For stickier residues like protein powder, let it soak for a minute before running the cycle. The container is also dishwasher-safe on the top rack.
