15 — faq

What is the difference between the A3500 and the E310?
The A3500 is a premium Ascent Series model with touchscreen controls, five pre-programmed settings, wireless connectivity, and Self-Detect technology. The E310 is an Explorian Series model with manual variable speed control and a smaller 48 oz container. Both share the same powerful motor lineage and come with a full 10-year warranty.
Can a Vitamix really make hot soup?
Yes. The blades spin fast enough to generate friction heat, bringing cold ingredients to steaming-hot soup temperature in about 6 to 10 minutes. The Hot Soup program on the A3500 automates the process completely.
What does the 10-year warranty cover?
The full 10-year warranty covers the motor, the drive system, and the container for normal household use. If anything fails under normal use during that period, Vitamix will repair or replace it at no charge. This is one of the longest warranties in the kitchen appliance industry.
How loud is a Vitamix blender?
At maximum speed, a Vitamix measures around 88 dB, which is comparable to a food processor or garbage disposal. At lower speeds, it is significantly quieter. The Ascent Series models with low-profile 64 oz containers tend to be slightly quieter than Classic Series machines due to improved sound dampening.

18 — troubleshooting-steps

Maintenance

How to Self-Clean Your Vitamix

1

Rinse Immediately

After each use, rinse the container with warm water to remove large food particles. Do not let residue dry on the walls or blades.

2

Fill Halfway with Warm Water

Add warm water until the container is about half full. Add a drop of dish soap. The water level ensures proper blade contact for cleaning.

3

Run on High for 60 Seconds

Secure the lid, start on variable speed 1, and quickly increase to high. Let it run for 30 to 60 seconds. The vortex cleans every surface the blades can reach.

4

Rinse and Air Dry

Pour out the soapy water, rinse thoroughly with clean water, and set the container upside-down on a drying mat. It will be ready for next time.

19 — allergen-safety

Safety & Allergens

Allergen-Safe Blending

Guidelines for households managing food allergies and sensitivities.

Cleaning Protocol

Run a full self-cleaning cycle with hot water and dish soap between allergen and allergen-free batches. For thorough sanitation, disassemble the lid and gasket and wash each part individually.

Dedicated Containers

For households with severe allergies, consider purchasing a second Ascent Series container. Label one for allergen-containing recipes and one for allergen-free. Containers swap in seconds.

Material Safety

All Vitamix containers are made from BPA-free Eastman Tritan copolyester. Blades are stainless steel. No latex, phthalates, or lead are used in any food-contact surface.

Always consult with a healthcare professional regarding specific allergen management. Vitamix containers are not certified allergen-free environments.
